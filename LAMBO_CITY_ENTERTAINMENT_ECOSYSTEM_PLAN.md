# LAMBO CITY ENTERTAINMENT ECOSYSTEM — Product & Development Plan

**Status:** Strategic direction approved; expansion is phased
**Role in the ecosystem:** The connective layer for music, creators, events, experiences, and future partnerships

## Vision

LAMBO CITY can grow beyond a single game or storefront into a connected digital entertainment ecosystem\. Music, interactive spaces, events, creators, and commerce should reinforce one another while remaining independently maintainable\.

## Potential experiences

- Music listening experiences and artist/label showcases\.
- Live or scheduled digital events and performances\.
- Venues, lounges, studios, galleries, and creator spaces\.
- Creator profiles and approved collaborations\.
- Event merchandise, music releases, and digital products\.
- Partnerships and branded experiences that fit LAMBO CITY’s identity\.

## Relationship to existing products

- The LAMBO CITY World provides the immersive locations and interaction layer\.
- LAMBO CITY Studio provides music creation and production workflows\.
- LAMBO CITY Commerce provides product discovery, checkout, and fulfillment\.
- Digital Real Estate may provide spaces for creators, businesses, and events\.

These systems should connect through explicit interfaces\. None should require all the others to be online or active for its core functions to work\.

## Architecture principles

- Preserve the user’s control over the LAMBO CITY brand, creative direction, and release decisions\.
- Keep music rights, licenses, permissions, and attribution clear\.
- Do not use third\-party audio or samples unless properly licensed and approved\.
- Design events and creator features as optional modules\.
- Avoid introducing accounts, multiplayer, live streaming, or user\-generated\-content hosting until the operational, moderation, privacy, safety, and cost requirements are understood\.
- Do not make promises about audience size, revenue, ownership, or availability that the product cannot guarantee\.

## Phased roadmap

1. **Planning now:** Establish the ecosystem vision and how the four pillars connect\.
2. **Current build:** Complete the immersive world and shared Studio foundation\.
3. **Later prototypes:** Add curated music experiences and non\-commercial event concepts\.
4. **Operational review:** Define rights, creator agreements, moderation, event operations, privacy, and support\.
5. **Expansion:** Introduce partnerships and additional creator/business experiences only when the core systems can support them\.

## Non\-goals for the current phase

- No live event platform or streaming infrastructure\.
- No open creator marketplace\.
- No automatic music distribution or rights grants\.
- No AI runtime agents in the game\.
- No paid integrations or third\-party services without approval\.
