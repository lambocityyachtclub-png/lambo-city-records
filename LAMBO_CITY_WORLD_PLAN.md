LAMBO CITY WORLD — Product & Development Plan

Status: Strategic direction approved; implementation remains phased
Role in the ecosystem: The immersive digital world and primary experience layer

Vision

LAMBO CITY is an immersive, explorable digital environment—not simply a game with a shop attached. The world should feel like a believable place with functional locations, recognizable destinations, useful interactions, and room to grow into a larger entertainment ecosystem.

Experience pillars

• Explore a coherent city and waterfront environment.
• Visit buildings, studios, venues, businesses, and future digital properties.
• Interact with NPCs and environmental objects in purposeful ways.
• Experience events and music-related activities as those systems are developed.
• Support touch-first iPad/mobile interaction while remaining usable with mouse, trackpad, and desktop controls.
• Maintain the established luxury LAMBO CITY visual identity and immersive realism.

Architecture principles

• Keep the world/game engine independent from commerce, payment processing, and property transactions.
• Connect future modules through clearly defined interfaces rather than embedding business logic throughout world files.
• Preserve working world systems when adding new features.
• Keep Phase 1 single-player. Do not introduce accounts, multiplayer, online persistence, crypto, or an in-game economy unless separately approved.
• Do not make the world dependent on the standalone Studio or a third-party commerce provider.

Phased roadmap

1. Current priority: Finish and verify the core world, movement, interactions, locations, and Floor 2 Studio experience.
2. Foundation: Document stable interfaces for locations, interactables, events, and future module entry points.
3. Later: Add optional experiences that connect to commerce, events, creators, and digital real estate.
4. Expansion: Consider multiplayer or persistent shared-world capabilities only after a separate technical, cost, safety, and product review.

Non-goals for the current phase

• No payment collection inside the game.
• No cryptocurrency, token, or blockchain dependency.
• No accounts/backend or multiplayer implementation.
• No changes that compromise existing working game systems.

Acceptance principles

A new world feature should be understandable, testable, modular, performant on supported devices, and usable without requiring a purchase.
