LAMBO CITY DIGITAL REAL ESTATE — Product & Development Plan
Status: Long-term concept approved for planning; no sale or ownership system authorized yet.
Role in the ecosystem: Digital spaces and property experiences within LAMBO CITY.
1. Vision
LAMBO CITY may eventually offer digital properties and spaces that people can acquire, personalize, access, operate, or use for experiences inside the world.
The product should first establish meaningful utility and a clear user experience. A property should be a functional, explorable place—not merely a label or speculative asset.
2. Potential Property Experiences
Homes, apartments, studios, offices, retail spaces, venues, and waterfront properties.
Interior customization and furnishing.
Business or creator use of eligible spaces.
Invitations, access permissions, and visitor experiences.
Optional rental, licensing, or transfer models if later approved and legally reviewed.
3. Ownership Model Must Be Decided Before Implementation
“Own” can mean different things: a license to use a space, an account-based entitlement, a transferable contractual right, or another legal structure.
The exact rights, limits, transferability, permanence, and remedies must be defined clearly before anything is offered for sale.
Do not assume that blockchain, NFTs, tokens, or cryptocurrency are required. Start with the simplest reliable model that provides the intended customer rights and can be supported operationally.
4. Architecture Boundaries
Keep property catalog, entitlements, access rules, and transaction records separate from the rendering/world engine.
The world should display and interact with properties through stable interfaces.
The property system must not be required for ordinary exploration or the existing single-player experience.
Protect customer records and access permissions.
Design for portability and clear export/recovery paths where practical.
5. Phased Roadmap
Planning now: Define property types, intended utility, and possible customer rights.
World foundation: Continue building believable locations and reusable building/interior systems without selling property.
Prototype later: Create a non-commercial demo of property selection, customization, and access.
Before any offer: Obtain legal, tax, consumer-protection, privacy, and platform-term review; define support and dispute processes.
Launch only after approval: Implement the selected entitlement and transaction model.
6. Non-Goals for the Current Phase
No property sales, token issuance, blockchain, or crypto.
No claims of real-world land ownership.
No paid property reservations or presales.
No transfer, rental, or resale promises before rights and rules are defined.
