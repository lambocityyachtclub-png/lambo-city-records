# ALIVE + LAMBO CITY — Brand and Experience System
Version 1.2 · Creative direction standard · 27 November 2026

## Brand ambition
Create a luxury digital universe with the emotional impact of a supercar reveal, the craftsmanship of a maison, and the cultural energy of a contemporary art studio. The work must feel authored, collectible, cinematic, and unmistakably original.

LAMBO CITY is the destination: a living entertainment, culture, and creative world.
ALIVE is the personal identity and Companion layer: the person, their creations, their history, and the tools that travel with them.
HERO is the creative host and record-making guide inside the LAMBO CITY experience.

## Permanent creative reference pillars
The private creative direction explicitly studies three reference points:

1. **Lamborghini — performance, precision, and presence.** Use the reference to guide dramatic original silhouettes, engineering-led proportions, responsive movement, acceleration, material precision, deep reflections, and the feeling of power. The target is not merely a luxury appearance; performance must be felt in the way the world moves and responds.
2. **Louis Vuitton — luxury craftsmanship and experience.** Use the reference to guide exceptional finish, architectural presentation, fashion-minded curation, exclusivity, consistency, tactile detail, and the sense that every interaction belongs to a considered premium experience.
3. **Takashi Murakami — expressive art and imagination.** Use the reference to guide the selective use of bold color, playful original character design, expressive clothing and accessories, collectible artistic treatments, and curated visual moments. This expression should be strongest in Companions, fashion, skins, stage packages, and selected digital media—not across the entire world.

The intended synthesis is a photorealistic luxury universe with the performance and precision associated with Lamborghini, the crafted experience associated with Louis Vuitton, and carefully placed original artistic expression informed by Takashi Murakami. Luxury remains the foundation; art is the expression; performance is the engine; ALIVE connects everything.

These are internal creative references and inspiration principles—not partnerships, permissions, sponsorships, or endorsements. They belong in internal planning, design briefs, and project documentation so the team understands the ambition. Public-facing website copy, advertising, product branding, and promotional materials must not use these names or imply any relationship unless separately authorized. All shipped product identity, logos, symbols, characters, clothing, patterns, artwork, and assets must remain original or properly licensed. Do not reproduce third-party logos, monograms, signature motifs, trade dress, protected artwork, or recognizable characters.

## The central design philosophy
**Luxury is the foundation. Art is the expression. Performance is the engine. ALIVE connects everything.**

The world should remain photorealistic, tactile, architectural, and premium. Expressive color and playful art are deliberate accents, not an all-over cartoon treatment. Artistic personality belongs most strongly in Companion characters, clothing, skins, accessories, selected stage environments, artist content, and curated billboards. It must never overwhelm the consistent luxury foundation.

## Design principles
1. **Iconic silhouette before decoration.** Every major interface or building needs one memorable shape or composition that reads at a distance.
2. **Craftsmanship at every scale.** Typography, spacing, material response, motion, sound, stitching, reflections, and micro-interactions should feel intentional.
3. **Controlled surprise.** The base system is restrained and architectural; expressive art, color, and collectible moments appear as curated accents.
4. **One visual universe, distinct signatures.** LAMBO CITY owns the city, entertainment, and culture. ALIVE owns identity, Companion, and personal continuity. Shared foundations, different signatures.
5. **Luxury is restraint plus access.** Do not fill every surface with gold, badges, gradients, or animation. Use negative space, hierarchy, tactile detail, and deliberate reveal.
6. **Performance is visible in the details.** Precision, responsive movement, reflective finishes, believable materials, and smooth interactions should communicate a powerful underlying engine.
7. **Originality is mandatory.** Do not reproduce third-party logos, monograms, signature motifs, trade dress, protected artwork, or recognizable characters. Translate broad principles into original geometry, typography, symbols, and characters.

## Visual architecture
### Core palette
- Obsidian: #090A0D — primary stage and deep surfaces.
- Graphite: #171A20 — structural panels and architectural shadows.
- Porcelain: #F2EFE8 — editorial light surfaces and readable text.
- Champagne: #D6BD86 — precision accent, never a full-page fill.
- Signal Lime: #C7F36B — ALIVE active-state signature, used sparingly.
- Ultraviolet: #8D7BFF — art/culture accent for curated drops.
- Deep Petrol: #173E43 — environmental and water-side accent.

The palette is a foundation, not a limit. Artist-selected accents may introduce vivid hues in clearly designated creative surfaces while preserving readable contrast and the surrounding material language.

### Typography
- Display: a high-contrast editorial serif or custom display face for campaign headlines and moments of arrival.
- Interface: a neutral modern sans with excellent small-size legibility.
- Numeric/technical: tabular monospace for specs, status, build versions, and ownership records.
- Avoid faux-Italian naming, excessive all-caps, and generic futuristic sci-fi fonts.

### Shape language
Develop an original **Orbit / Cutline** geometry: an open circular orbit intersected by one decisive diagonal cut. Use it as a recurring navigation, loading, seal, and ownership motif. Pair with controlled angular chamfers and rounded interaction surfaces. Do not borrow recognizable third-party signature geometry as a logo system.

### Material language
- Satin black anodized metal, smoked glass, porcelain, brushed champagne metal, carbon-fiber-inspired microtexture, and deep petrol glass.
- Use physically plausible highlights, roughness variation, bevels, reflections, and shadow depth. Avoid plastic gold, noisy textures, and excessive bloom.
- Clothing and character materials should show believable fabric weave, seams, stitching, leather, metal hardware, and physically plausible folds.
- In the 3D world, prioritize baked/emissive accents and a small real-light budget. Preserve approved geometry and camera/navigation.

### Motion
Motion should feel like precision machinery and an art installation: short, weighted, and purposeful. Use a reveal, settle, and subtle idle state. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep iPad frame budgets.

## Artistic expression and customization
### Companion characters, clothing, and skins
Characters are the primary canvas for personal artistic expression. Keep human proportions, believable anatomy, realistic materials, and cinematic lighting. Allow individuality through:
- Original clothing collections, statement jackets, tailored pieces, streetwear, footwear, and accessories.
- Distinctive but curated patterns, graphic treatments, colorways, and character skins.
- Limited or seasonal collections and creator collaborations using original commissioned assets.
- Companion styling that can evolve independently of the person's underlying ALIVE identity.

Art should feel integrated into fashion and character design, not pasted on as noisy decoration. Provide restrained everyday options alongside expressive statement looks. Users should be able to express themselves without making the shared world visually chaotic.

### Artist-configurable stage experiences
Treat the stage as a stable, modular venue with configurable creative layers. Artists may select approved options for:
- Stage visual package and backdrop artwork.
- LED screen content and animation.
- Stage-floor color and lighting treatment.
- Accent palette, lighting intensity, and event graphics.
- Approved modular scenic elements.

The artist changes the atmosphere, not the shared city's permanent structure. Keep safety, sightlines, performance, audio synchronization, and accessibility consistent. Stage configurations must be data-driven and reversible rather than one-off rebuilds.

### Billboards and digital media
Billboards should attract attention through art direction, composition, color, scale, and motion quality—not flashing or visual noise. Support original campaign artwork, high-resolution media, controlled animation, realistic screen brightness, and believable reflections. Preserve legibility and the architectural context around each display.

### Creative boundaries
Create designated expressive surfaces and configurable zones. Do not let artist content overwrite shared architecture, roads, approved world geography, navigation, or another creator's work. All uploaded or commissioned assets need rights clearance, moderation rules, safe rendering constraints, and a clear owner/creator attribution model.

## Experience architecture
- **LAMBO CITY:** the destination, world, events, culture, and shared experiences.
- **ALIVE:** portable personal identity, Companion, preferences, saved work, privacy, and export.
- **HERO:** founder identity, in-world host, and guided creative workflow for making records.
- **Experience Customization System:** reusable configurations for clothing, Companion skins, stage visuals, billboard media, event palettes, and other approved creative surfaces.
- **Records / Creations:** user-owned works with explicit save, version history, and export paths.
- **Owner Control:** private founder controls, Living Contracts, audit history, and deployment approval.

## ALIVE as the high-performance experience engine
ALIVE is not merely a chatbot or a decorative label. It is the shared identity and experience layer that connects people, Companions, creation tools, social permissions, events, and personal continuity across LAMBO CITY.

Build the technical foundation as modular services and interfaces:
- Identity, profile, Companion, and owner-controlled preferences.
- Social graph, consent, presence, and privacy-aware communication.
- Creation records, versions, ownership, and export.
- Event and experience configuration.
- Shared rendering and interaction contracts for the world.
- Auditable permissions and deployment boundaries.

The engine must be responsive, dependable, and efficient. Performance work is part of the brand: smooth movement, fast interactions, stable frame rates, realistic materials, and reliable state continuity. Do not claim capabilities are live until they are implemented and tested end to end.

## Modular world-production method
Build one independently scoped world section at a time: for example, Jumbotron/stage, each store, each yacht, Yacht Club Towers, Convention Center, villas, boardwalk, and recreation areas.

Every section receives a complete blueprint before implementation:
1. Purpose, audience, and experience flow.
2. Approved location, footprint, scale, and connection points.
3. Architecture, proportions, materials, reflections, and lighting.
4. Interactive behavior, social permissions, and creator controls.
5. Performance budget for iPad and desktop.
6. Dependencies and existing systems to reuse.
7. Test plan, preview route, rollback boundary, and acceptance criteria.

Implement each section as a modular feature with explicit initialization, update, and shared-context contracts. Reuse existing assets and systems before creating duplicates. Preserve approved world geography, object positions, camera, walking paths, joystick/WASD, locked HERO performance, and working production features. Do not rebuild the entire project to upgrade one section.

The production loop is: inspect → blueprint → implement → test → Preview → founder review → explicit production approval. A completed section is locked against unrelated changes while the next section is developed.

## Social experience
LAMBO CITY should feel like a living social destination, not simply a 3D map. People arrive through their ALIVE identity and Companion, meet friends, attend events, explore destinations, discover creators, and participate in activities.

Build toward:
- Persistent personal identity and Companion presence.
- User-controlled profiles, outfits, and social visibility.
- Consent-based proximity voice and friend communication.
- Events, performances, invitations, and creator spaces.
- Interactive retail, recreation, and shared activities.
- Creator publishing and collaborations with clear ownership and moderation.

Privacy, consent, safety, and data portability are foundational requirements. Do not expose a user's real camera, microphone, personal information, or saved creations without explicit permission and clear controls.

## Quality gates
A release is not luxury-ready unless it passes:
- Brand consistency across desktop and iPad.
- Clear first action and understandable navigation.
- Keyboard, touch, contrast, and reduced-motion accessibility.
- Fast initial load and no unnecessary heavy framework.
- No unapproved changes to world geometry, camera, walking paths, HERO performance, or production auth.
- Clear ownership, save status, export availability, and privacy language for personal data.
- Tested loading, error, empty, offline, and recovery states.
- No visual noise, oversaturated default environments, or unlicensed third-party assets.
- Modular tests proving a section can be changed without damaging unrelated sections.

## Creative direction
The experience should feel like entering a private creative club, not filling out a SaaS form. Use cinematic entry moments, architectural framing, editorial storytelling, bespoke iconography, carefully curated color, and collectible content. The Companion must become a recognizable original character system over time. Maintain a realistic luxury world while allowing fashion, characters, artist stages, and selected media surfaces to carry more expressive personality.

## Release rule
Ship one coherent section at a time to Preview. A section advances only when its experience, architecture, accessibility, performance, ownership model, and rollback plan are verified. Production always requires explicit owner approval.
